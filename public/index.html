View required fields and database-compatible format
ColumnFormat / rule
referenceUnique sale ID, maximum 80 characters. Repeat for every item belonging to one sale.
sold_atYYYY-MM-DD HH:mm, Philippine local time (e.g. 2026-10-30 14:30).
branch_nameMust match an existing branch name.
statusSold, Voided, or Cancelled. Unpaid/pending cannot be imported into this sales table.
payment_modeCash or GCash (supported by the current database constraint).
item_nameProduct name, 1–150 characters, one row per item.
quantityPositive number, at most 3 decimal places.
unit_priceNon-negative number, at most 2 decimal places, without currency symbol or commas.
sale_totalRepeated total for this sale: sum of item quantities × unit prices minus discount.
Optional columns: customer_name, cashier_name, order_type (Dine In / Take Away / Order Online), discount_amount, payment_reference, remark.

Imports do not affect current stock. Historical recipe costs cannot be reconstructed from sales totals, so legacy profit/costing figures require separate verification. Existing sales are never overwritten.
